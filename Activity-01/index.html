Golden Garlic Pasta

A cozy, creamy garlic pasta that's quick to make and packed with buttery, savory flavor.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/4 cup pasta water
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water until tender, then reserve 1/4 cup of the pasta water before draining.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat and sauté the minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the drained spaghetti to the pan and toss until evenly coated with the garlic butter.
  4. Pour in the reserved pasta water and sprinkle in the Parmesan cheese.
  5. Toss everything together until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper, then garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.
